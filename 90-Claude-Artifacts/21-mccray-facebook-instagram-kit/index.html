Animated Reels · 1080 × 1920

Show it in motion

20-second animated story previews for Reels & Stories. Every screen moves — staggered titles, Ken Burns art, word-by-word captions — and each one lands the app’s signature beat: the touch taps the not-quite choice, it reddens and shakes under a “↻ Not quite — try again!” toast, then taps the good one for gold + a ✓ stamp and sparkles. The retry is the point — a wrong answer is safe, and the app answers back. Branded CTA + a “Follow for a new story every week” pill, over a soft music bed.

My Brave Friends ships in two editions

One codebase, two builds from build-editions.mjs, selected per Netlify site by the MBF_EDITION variable. A missing or invalid value resolves to consumer, so a misconfiguration can only over-withhold, never leak.

  • Consumer — mybravefriends.com. Stories 30, 32, 33 are withheld at build time: their prose is absent from the file, replaced by an inert stub that can never be unlocked. Story 31 ships reframed (a neutral startle variant). Stories 34 and 35 are present and unlock through the Grown-ups panel.
  • Counselor — counselor.mybravefriends.com, password-walled. All 36 stories including the full hardship prose and the trauma-framed original of 31.

The withholding test is “does the story name a threat the child may not know exists” — not “is the topic hard.” That is why 24, 27 and 29 (poverty teasing, food insecurity, housing loss) stay in the consumer build: gating them would put them furthest from the children they were written for.

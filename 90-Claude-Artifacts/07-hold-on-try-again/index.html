Hold On, Try Again

A big-energy scene for excitement that needs somewhere to go. Mistiming it is never a fail state — it's just another lap of the same ring.

Watch the ring flash pink, then tap Hold On.
HOLDS
0/3
Input

A timed tap, not a rushed one. The ring flashes pink for a real window each lap — tap inside it, not just fast.

State

Three holds needed. A mistimed tap doesn't cost anything except one more lap of the same ring — there's no wrong-answer state here, only not-yet.

Output

A good hold pops a visible starburst and advances the count. All the energy of the scene stays — it's channeled, not calmed.

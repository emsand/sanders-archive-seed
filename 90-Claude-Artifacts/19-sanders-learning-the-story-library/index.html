A few notes for grown-ups

Word Power. Every story for children builds one word — named, defined and earned as a badge at the end. The Spanish word is not a translation bolted on; both languages are written together.

Grown-Up Practice. In My Whole Story, twenty-five of the stories put the adult in the choice seat: your child comes home carrying something hard, and you decide what to say first. If the first thing you would say isn't the one that helps, you simply try again.

Stories marked “grown-up opt-in” touch on tender ground — a hard feeling, a family worry, a person who is far away. They stay switched off until a grown-up turns them on, so a family or a classroom meets them when it is ready.

A practice tool, not therapy. These stories are for rehearsing a feeling before it happens for real. They don't diagnose anything and they aren't a substitute for care from a counselor or clinician.

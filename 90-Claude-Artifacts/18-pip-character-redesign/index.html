The McCray Learning Lab · design decision

Pip becomes an owlet

Pip and Sprout had become the same character. This is what was wrong, what was tested, and what was approved.

Approved 29 July 2026
Mint owlet — smiling variant

Art brief written into PILOT-PIP-REDESIGN.md. Nothing has been regenerated yet.

The problem

Reduced to flat black shapes — the standard test for whether two characters read apart — Pip and Sprout are the same silhouette: a circle, a two-leaf stem, two stubby legs. The bow tie was the only thing separating them.

That matters most for the children this is built for. Pre-readers can't read “Pip” or “Sprout” to recover the difference, so a bow tie was carrying the entire job of telling two guides apart. Colour alone couldn't fix it, because the silhouettes were identical.

The leaves were never Pip's Pip's own canon describes “the peace-sprite”, “an original spirit guide”, the “breathing orb” who “floats, glows and beckons” — and “Pip is glowing!” is a story beat. The seedling belongs to Sprout: growth, trying again. Removing it brings the art back in line with the character as written. This is not a change of character.

What was tested

Two rounds. The first produced a lantern and a crescent moon — both passed the shape test and both were rejected, because they're objects. Bloom, Sprout and Auntie Nia all have faces and agency; an object would have made Pip the one guide who can't look back at a child.

The second round tested five creatures. A firefly failed twice — first reading as a lumpy mass like Bloom, then, redrawn, as two leaves on a stem like Sprout. An elephant calf stayed too close to Bloom's wide silhouette. The owlet, a floppy-eared pup, a glowing jelly and a little whale all passed.

The approved design

The owlet keeps everything the canon needs. It perches and flies, so Pip still floats. It's a night creature, so the glow stays native. Its eyes carry the bonding. And “watchful, unhurried” matches Pip's existing line about looking slow, on purpose.

The mint does double duty: it holds the four-guide colour system together and pulls Pip's hue away from Sprout's yellow-green, so colour reinforces the new silhouette instead of fighting it.

What has to be regenerated

  • Four guide-intro videos — pip-guide-intro.mp4 in the conflict-resolution, conflict-grownups, empathy and upstander sites.
  • Cast and marketing art — assets/hero-cast.webp, the derived clean crop, and both YouTube thumbnails.
  • Signature-song videos — Pip's own, plus the four-guide row in the other three.
Extraction note The guides sit closer together on the cast sheet than any safe crop margin, so sprites must be pulled out by connected component — flood-fill the character's own pixels — never by rectangle. A rectangle wide enough to avoid clipping one guide always drags in a slice of the next. That's what clipped Sprout's leaf and left it shipping cut off.

The 🌱 → ✨ copy change

Pip's interface currently borrows Sprout's seedling. It becomes a glow — but the swap has to be surgical.

Do not find-and-replace Most 🌱 across the sites belong to Sprout, where the seedling is correct and stays. A blanket replace would strip Sprout's own metaphor. Only the eight Pip-specific instances change, listed by file and line in the brief.

Two further lines are flagged for a human decision rather than asserted: both render <span class="pbar-pip">🌱</span>, where “pip” means a progress-bar pip — a UI dot, not the character. The same markup appears on Sprout's sites, so it needs reading in context.

Status

The art brief, expression set, regeneration list and copy changes are written into PILOT-PIP-REDESIGN.md, and the approved art is saved at assets/brand/pip-owlet-concept.png. Nothing has been regenerated. The live sites still show the old green sprite.

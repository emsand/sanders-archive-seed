The Quiet Deep

A breathing scene where the child's own calm is the light source. Not a decoration on top of a breathing exercise — the breathing is what brightens the world.

Tap begin, and breathe with her.
CALM
0/3
Input

Same house pattern as the rest of the library — inhale 4s, exhale 6s, no hold. The long exhale is the calming part; a hold would raise tension.

State

Each completed cycle is one step on a 3-step calm meter. It only advances on a full, paced cycle — rushing or bailing early doesn't count.

Output

Calm state drives everything visible: the vignette lifts, the pendant brightens, one shy light appears per step. Nothing unlocks on a timer or by chance.

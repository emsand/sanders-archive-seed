One Steady Step

A courage scene for a new or unfamiliar place. Brave doesn't mean not-nervous — it means one steady step, taken anyway, four times over.

Tap to take a step. Wait for steady, then go.
STEPS 0/4
Input

A tap, but every tap opens a brief steady beat first. Tapping again inside that beat doesn't skip it — speed isn't the point.

State

Four steps, each requiring its own completed beat. Steps: 0–4 — a plain, countable measure of steady forward motion.

Output

Each step lights one marker on the ground and the doorway ahead brightens — the destination gets visibly closer with every steady step, not sooner.

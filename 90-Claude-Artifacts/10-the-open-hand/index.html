The Open Hand

A patience scene where staying still, not reaching, is what earns trust. Grabbing never works — only an unhurried, unbroken hold does.

Press and hold. Don't let go — let her decide when to come.
TRUST
0/3
Input

A press-and-hold, not a tap. The instruction is stillness itself — the child's own patience is the input being practiced.

State

Trust only rises on a full, uninterrupted 2.5s hold. Letting go early doesn't erase progress already earned — it just asks again.

Output

Each complete hold visibly draws the creature one step closer. Nothing is startled away for good; nothing is timed against the child.
